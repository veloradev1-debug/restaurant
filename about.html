Our Story

A Legacy of Taste

Elysian Fields was born from a vision to create a sanctuary for food lovers — a place where every meal is a celebration and every guest is royalty. Our name, inspired by the paradise of heroes in Greek mythology, reflects our aspiration to offer a dining experience that is nothing short of heavenly.

Founded by world-renowned Chef Antoine Dubois, our kitchen is a laboratory of innovation, deeply rooted in classic techniques. We believe in the poetry of food, where the finest ingredients are transformed into masterpieces of flavor and presentation.
